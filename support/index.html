Anima Grid Support

Need help with Anima Grid? Email us and describe what happened. We will use the information you provide only to investigate and respond to your request.

support@animagrid.com

Helpful details to include

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The Anima Grid app version.
  • The screen or action where the issue occurred.
  • A short description of what you expected and what happened.

Never email passwords, authentication credentials, payment details, or other secrets.

Privacy and data requests

You can use the same address for privacy questions or deletion requests. Read the Anima Grid Privacy Policy for details.
